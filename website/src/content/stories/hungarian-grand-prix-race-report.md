Lando Norris secured his and McLaren's first win of the 2026 season with a brilliant drive at the Hungarian Grand Prix, bouncing back from an early race scare to dominate the field. 

Norris started on pole, but teammate Oscar Piastri snatched the lead with a clever cut-back move at Turn 2 on the opening lap. The two McLarens ran first and second for much of the first half, engaging in a tense intra-team battle while managing tire strategies.

The race flipped completely after the midway point. While navigating blue flags in traffic, Piastri collided with Carlos Sainz at Turn 2, heavily damaging his momentum. Capitalizing on superior clean-air pace and longer stints, Norris surged ahead to claim a net lead. Piastri's afternoon ended in total heartbreak shortly after when a sudden gearbox failure forced him to retire.

A late Virtual Safety Car triggered a flurry of pit activity. Red Bull's Max Verstappen and Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli seized the opportunity by staying out, securing second and third places respectively.

Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton initially crossed the line ahead of Antonelli, but a late five-second penalty for pit-lane speeding dropped him behind teammate Charles Leclerc to fifth. George Russell completed an impressive recovery drive to finish seventh after a disastrous anti-stall start dropped him all the way to 21st.

Meanwhile, Isack Hadjar finished sixth for Red Bull, and Liam Lawson grabbed eighth ahead of Nico Hulkenberg and Arvid Lindblad, who rounded out the points. Aside from Piastri, Cadillac endured a rough day with both Valtteri Bottas and Sergio Perez retiring due to mechanical issues.

Norris crossed the finish line 15 seconds clear of Verstappen, celebrating a sensational victory before Formula 1 heads into its summer break ahead of the Dutch Grand Prix.
