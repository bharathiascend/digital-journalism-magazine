# Why Student Journalism Matters

**Subtitle:** The importance of young voices in shaping conversations and exploring the world around us.

Journalism is more than reporting events. It is about observing society, asking meaningful questions, understanding different perspectives, and communicating ideas responsibly.

For students, journalism creates an opportunity to look beyond textbooks and classrooms. A student journalist can explore campus issues, cultural changes, public discussions, and everyday experiences that deserve attention.

Digital platforms have made publishing more accessible. Students can combine written articles, photographs, videos, and podcasts to tell stories in different formats.

However, accessibility also brings responsibility. Good journalism requires accuracy, fairness, verification, and respect for the people whose stories are being told.

GLASNOST represents an opportunity for student voices to participate in these conversations. Through writing and multimedia storytelling, young journalists can practise expressing ideas clearly while developing their understanding of society.

Student journalism matters because learning to ask the right questions is just as important as learning to communicate the answers.
