Host Mithraa leads an investigative deep dive into the 'Pink Tax' in urban Indian retail, auditing dozens of personal care items where women's products cost up to 30% more than identical men's variants.

## Episode Chapters

- **00:00 - 05:20:** The retail audit: side-by-side pricing of razors, deodorants, and haircuts.
- **05:21 - 12:45:** The economic justifications versus consumer exploitation.
- **12:46 - 21:50:** Regulatory frameworks and consumer advocacy for price parity.
